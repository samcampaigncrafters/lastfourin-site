How it works 💡

  1. Open a race room and send the link. Up to 7 friends can join.
  2. When everyone's in, the host presses start. A short countdown, then the same list and the same clock for everyone.
  3. Type a full name, or a last name once only one open name has it. Get there first and the name is yours, in your colour on every screen.
  4. Most names wins. In private rooms, a tie goes to whoever got their last one first. New ranked trophy games use a fresh sudden-death prompt when regulation ends level. Lists come from the sandbox, so races never touch your daily record or streak.
