Pin Drop 📍

Five football places a day: a stadium, a college town, a Super Bowl, where a star played college ball, where a star was born. Drop a pin. The closer you land, the more you score.

Your streak 🔥

How it works 💡

  1. A new set of five drops every night at midnight Eastern, the same for everyone.
  2. Spin the globe and tap to drop your pin. Tap again to move it, zoom in for the close calls, then lock it in.
  3. Up to 1,000 points a round: a pin within 10 miles is worth almost all of it, 300 miles off is about a third.
  4. Stadiums go by today's names, even for old Super Bowls. College rounds count the team's home stadium.
  5. Share your score as a row of pins, no answers given away.
