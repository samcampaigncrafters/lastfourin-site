How it works

  1. Every week, everyone starts with $1,000 in pretend money. It's free and there's nothing to buy.
  2. Bet spreads, totals and moneylines on real games in every league on the site. Lines come from ESPN and lock at kickoff.
  3. Weeks run Tuesday to Monday. You can bet any game that kicks off before the Tuesday 4am ET reset.
  4. Bets settle on their own when the game goes final. Postponed games hand your stake back.
  5. Most money when the week resets wins bragging rights. No real money in and no prize out.
  6. Go broke and you can start over for the rest of the week, but only your first run counts on the Everyone board.
