Bubble AI Power ratings

    How it works

    1. Every team gets an offense and a defense rating from this season's final scores, adjusted for who they played. Last season counts a little early on and fades out.
    2. For each game the model projects both scores, adds home field, and docks a team starting a backup quarterback.
    3. It compares its line with the market's. Where they disagree it leans one way, and the bigger the gap, the more bars it gets.
    4. A very big gap caps the confidence: the market may know about an injury the model can't see.
    5. The percent is the model's chance the bet hits: results spread about 13.5 points around its margin and 10 around its total.
    6. Player props: each player's projection is 60% his season average and 40% his last three games, nudged for the defense he faces, then weighed against the posted line (the line gets a quarter of the say).
    7. Best Bets of the Day lists the picks where the model's chance beats the market's by the most on one game day, leaving out capped gaps, players just back from a missed stretch and touchdown or interception props.
