Privacy policy

Short version: we collect as little as we can, we never sell it, and you can delete your account and everything in it any time.

Without an account

Your picks, brackets and scores are saved in your own browser on your device. They aren't sent to us. Game data loads from public score feeds (ESPN), which may see your device's normal web request. We count page views with our own counter: it records the page, the site that linked you here, and an anonymous daily code made from your connection that can't be traced back to you and is reset every day. No cookies, and we never store your IP address.

With an account

  • Sign-in: your email address, and if you use Google, your name and profile picture from Google.
  • Profile: the handle, name and bio you choose. These are public.
  • Your activity: picks, entries, posts, comments, blocks and reports you make.
  • Age check: we ask your birth month and year once to confirm you're 13 or older. We keep only the fact that you passed, not your birthday.

What's public

Your handle, name, picture, bio, pick'em record and streaks, anything you post to your profile, and your comments. Your email is never shown. Other people's picks become visible only after their games kick off.

Who handles it

Accounts and data are stored with Supabase, our database provider. Google handles Google sign-in. We don't sell or rent your information, and we don't run ads that track you.

Children

Accounts are for people 13 and older. If someone under 13 tries to sign up, the account is deleted immediately and nothing is kept. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

Deleting your data

Go to My picks, then Delete account. That permanently removes your account, picks, posts, comments, blocks and reports.

Contact

Questions or requests: see the contact link on the homepage.
